Customer platform roadmap

Implemented foundation (feature/crm-member):
1) Branch-scoped CRM / Member profile.
2) Reservation-to-customer matching by normalized phone.
3) VIP, tier, birthday, tags, notes, preferred Sales/PR and visit summary.

Next planned major customer features:
1) Customer account access with LINE Login first.
2) Phone OTP second; email/magic link optional.
3) Member self-service profile and reservation history.
4) POS reconciliation against customer, Sales/PR and service session.

These features must build on Reservation + Floor Plan without changing confirmed table assignment semantics.
